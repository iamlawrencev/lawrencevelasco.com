## Who is Lawrence Velasco?

Lawrence Velasco is a software solutions developer and AI specialist based in the Philippines. He has worked in software development since 2016 and combines hands-on engineering with responsibility for scope, priorities, technical decisions, timelines, and delivery. His focus is not limited to one platform: he identifies the underlying problem, chooses an appropriate solution, and helps turn it into a maintainable product.

## How does Lawrence use AI in software delivery?

AI is a powerful engineering tool, but it does not automatically understand human intent, business reasoning, or why a decision was made. As projects grow, context rot and context drift make unstructured AI use less reliable.

Lawrence's [AI-enabled software delivery approach](https://lawrencevelasco.com/ai-enabled-software-delivery/) uses bounded tasks, durable context, explicit decisions, automated checks, and human review to keep AI-assisted work aligned with the real objective.
