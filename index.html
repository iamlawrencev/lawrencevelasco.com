About

The idea and implementation should stay connected.

I began my professional career in 2016 and built my foundation as a software developer before moving into managing software delivery. I still write and review code, which lets me work through implementation details without losing sight of the product’s purpose, the people involved, or the realities of delivering it.

Developer’s perspective

Because I have built software myself, I understand architecture, platform limitations, implementation details, maintainability, performance, and the technical tradeoffs that shape a product. That grounding helps me assess what is practical before work moves too far ahead.

Manager’s perspective

I also keep the original vision, scope, priorities, communication, constraints, and delivery decisions in view. Together, those perspectives help keep the idea and the implementation aligned as the work moves forward.
