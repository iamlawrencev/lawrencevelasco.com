About

A problem solver who can build the solution.

I started in 2016 as a software developer. Over time, I took on more responsibility for the project side — scope, priorities, timelines, and the conversations that turn an idea into something real. I still write and review code, but I also help teams make better decisions about what to build and how to get it shipped. I helped spark and now help lead my company's transition to AI, including the move to fully agentic workflows, with a focus on optimizing processes and making work better, faster, and easier for everyone involved.

My approach treats AI as a strong delivery tool, with bounded work, durable context, automated checks, and human judgment. Read how I structure AI-enabled software delivery →

Builder’s perspective

I still write and review code regularly, so I have a current sense of architecture, platform limits, and the tradeoffs that come with maintainability and performance. When someone's excited about an idea, I can usually tell early on what's going to be simple and what's going to be a slog.

Delivery perspective

On the other side, I'm the one thinking about scope, priorities, and what a client or team actually needs to hear about progress — including the parts nobody enjoys, like saying a deadline needs to move. Having both perspectives at once means fewer surprises for everyone by the time something ships.
